ESS digital twins

Welcome

You are about to operate a digital twin of ODIN, the neutron imaging instrument of the European Spallation Source: you set the instrument, measure, and analyze your own data, in this browser. Nothing to install.

Anonymous: no name is asked. This browser remembers your measurements and answers while the session is online: come back with the same browser to continue. At the end of the page, you can leave a comment on the exercise and submit your work. A page takes up to a minute to open.
