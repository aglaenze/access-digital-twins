Welcome

You are about to operate a digital twin of ODIN, the neutron imaging instrument of the European Spallation Source: you set the instrument, measure, and analyze your own data, in this browser. Nothing to install.

Your measurements and your answers are kept under your name while this session is online: use the same name if you come back, and download your work with Submit my work at the end of the page. A page takes up to a minute to open.
