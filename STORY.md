# The story of the twins: one world for all the exercises

*Approved on 2026-11-16; the missions of the pages, notebooks, sheets and cards follow it.* Every role and every mission of
the exercises (catalogue cards, BSc pages, MSc notebooks, beamtime sheets, proposals of the user office) follows this
file. A new exercise gets its place here first.

All the people, teams and companies of the story are **fictional** (names below are placeholders, easy to change). The
**samples are real**: they come from published work (Naver et al., Strobl et al., Makowska et al., Sales et al.,
Qvistgaard et al., the V20 measurement), always cited as literature, never as the clients of the story.

## The setting

**ESS, Lund.** The student joins the **Neutron Solutions Team**, a small team of ESS that takes problems from
companies and research groups and answers them with neutrons, on ODIN (imaging) and LoKI (small-angle scattering).
Each exercise is one **request** that arrives at the team. The clients come back from one exercise to the next: a
request often follows from the answer to an earlier one.

**People of the team**

| Who | Role in the story | Where they appear |
|---|---|---|
| **Maja Holm** | instrument scientist of ODIN, the student's mentor | introduction to ODIN (first day), the hidden fault (0 bis), the coil (7) |
| **Tomas Berg** | instrument scientist of LoKI | introduction to LoKI, the LoKI requests |
| **the student** | a new member of the team; their grade is their level (below) | everywhere |

**The three threads (the clients)**

1. **Energy: the Fyrø Energy Island consortium.** A (fictional) artificial island in the Kattegat that gathers the
   power of an offshore wind farm, turns part of it into hydrogen, stores part of it in batteries, and recycles the
   old turbines. Its departments send requests: *Structures* (steel, blades), *Hydrogen plant* (solid-oxide and PEM
   electrolysis), *Storage* (batteries), *Circularity* (recycling).
2. **Planets: the Mars team.** A (fictional) group of planetary scientists and paleomagnetists, with a collection of
   meteorites. Their question runs through several exercises: what do Martian rocks tell about the water and the
   magnetic field of early Mars?
3. **The instrument itself.** Requests from inside the team: a new coil for the polarized setup of ODIN, a fault to
   find before the next user arrives. They are what an instrument scientist does every day.

## The grades: the level is the student's role in the team

The level chosen in the catalogue is the student's grade. The client and the question stay the same at every level;
what changes is how much the student decides.

| Level | Grade in the team | What the client expects | In the pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BSc** | **Trainee**: Maja (or Tomas) has set up the instrument; you measure and give the answer | an answer: yes/no, a number, a map | "Maja has prepared the settings. The client needs..." |
| **MSc** | **Method scientist**: the client wants a method they can trust and repeat | a method, its uncertainty, what limits it | "The client will send many more samples: they need a method..." |
| **Beamtime** | **Lead scientist**: you wrote the proposal, it was accepted; you plan and run the whole beamtime in NICOS | a beamtime report, as a real user | the proposal sheet; the proposal in NICOS |
| (Free beamtime) | **Your own project**: your question, your samples | your own conclusions | — |

The MSc choice "with code / without code" does not change the role: the same method scientist, who reads the code of
each step or not.

### ODIN

**Introduction to ODIN: your first day.** ★ Maja shows you ODIN, from the source to the camera, before the first
requests arrive. (Same page; only its first paragraph is new: the welcome into the team.)

*Chapters 0 and 0 bis are hidden from the catalogue since 2026-11-16 (to be removed); Energy starts with the battery.*

**0. How far apart are the atoms in iron?** Thread: Energy (Fyrø *Structures*).
- Request ★: Fyrø's steel supplier has delivered plates for the foundations of the turbines, sold as ferritic steel
  (α-iron). A mix-up with austenitic steel would matter, and the plates may not be cut. Neutrons see the crystal
  structure through the steel.
- BSc: give your verdict on the plates: α-iron or not, and with which lattice parameter?
- MSc: Fyrø will check every delivery: build the analysis of a Bragg edge and give the verdict with its uncertainty.
- Beamtime (proposal 1234): before Fyrø's components are measured, show that ODIN measures the plane spacing of a
  reference plate reliably.
- (Today the BSc page says "a company that builds parts for research facilities" and the MSc notebook "a materials
  engineer": two different roles, unified here.)

**0 bis. Unknown metals (game).** Thread: Energy (Fyrø *Circularity*), then the instrument.
- Mission 1 ★: Fyrø's recycling yard takes apart old turbines; three plates have lost their labels, and a plate in
  the wrong furnace ruins a batch of alloy. Identify them, on a limited beamtime budget.
- Mission 2 ★: the same night, Maja is away and you are on duty: a user says their data look strange. Find the
  hidden fault of the instrument before the next user arrives.

**1. What is hidden inside a Martian meteorite?** Thread: Planets (the Mars team).
- Request: the Martian meteorite NWA 7034 holds water from early Mars. Where is it, and how much? A gram is worth
  more than gold: find the richest clast without cutting it, so that the team can drill it out. (Kept.)
- BSc: images, a tomography, find the clasts and their water.
- MSc: the team will receive more pieces: build a method to find water in 3D, with its uncertainty.
- Beamtime (proposal 1250): plan the tomography yourself.
- Opens chapter 7 (the same meteorite: after the water, the magnetic field).

**2. How does lithium enter a battery electrode?** Thread: Energy (Fyrø *Storage*).
- Request ★: the island stores the wind power of calm days in batteries. Thicker electrodes would store more energy
  in the same space, but does the lithium reach their far side, and how much is lost in the first cycle? Watch the
  lithium move inside a working cell. (Today: "a battery maker"; the battery maker becomes Fyrø's storage supplier.)
- BSc: film the cycle, read the profiles, answer the client.
- MSc: Fyrø wants to compare electrodes from several suppliers: a method from the raw images to lithium amounts
  comparable with the charge of the cycler.
- Beamtime (proposal 1260).

**3. Where is a loaded steel part strained?** Thread: Energy (Fyrø *Structures*).
- Request ★: a stainless steel part of the turbines carries a large force. Where, inside the steel, is it strained,
  and by how much? A strain gauge only sees the surface. (Today: "a team that designs steel parts for a wind
  turbine": the same team, now Fyrø's.) Follows from chapter 0: the same department, now with a part under load.
- BSc: strain in the middle and the ends, does the steel behave as designed?
- MSc: a method to measure lattice strain under load, its systematic errors, Poisson's ratio.
- Beamtime (proposal 1240).

**4. Where has the fuel-cell anode been reduced?** Thread: Energy (Fyrø *Hydrogen plant*).
- Request ★: the island turns wind power into hydrogen with stacks of solid-oxide cells. Before a stack runs,
  hydrogen turns the NiO of the anode supports into nickel, inside the stack, under load. Does the load make the
  reduction uneven? Map it without cutting the supports. (Today: "a company that builds stacks of solid-oxide cells":
  it becomes Fyrø's stack supplier, who sends the bars.)
- BSc, MSc, Beamtime (proposal 1280): as today.
- Title: "fuel-cell anode" stays true (the same cells run as fuel cells or electrolysers); the text says both.

**5. The crystals of an iron meteorite** (later). Thread: Planets (the Mars team's collection): the Cape York iron
meteorite, orientations of its large crystals.

**6. Structures smaller than a pixel** (later). Thread: the instrument. ODIN's grating interferometer (dark-field
imaging) is being set up: Maja asks you to show what it sees on a sample of known micrometer-sized structures, before
it is offered to the users. A setup for the polarized imaging comes next (chapter 7, the coil): the two new pieces of
equipment of the team. If the sample chosen with M. Strobl is an application (a fiber composite of the blades,
microcracks in concrete...), the request can move to Fyrø's thread instead.

**7. Seeing a magnetic field with neutrons.** Two samples, two threads, and they are linked:
- The coil ★ (thread: the instrument): the polarized setup of ODIN needs a new coil, built by the team's workshop.
  It must make about half a millitesla along its axis. Maja asks you to measure the field inside it without opening
  it. This is also the test of the method before it is used on a rock.
- The meteorite fragment ★ (thread: Planets): the Mars team comes back (chapter 1). A rock keeps a record of the
  field it formed in; a magnetometer only gives the total. Can neutrons show where the field sits inside a fragment
  of NWA 7034? Test it on a fragment magnetized on purpose.
- Beamtime (proposal 1270): both samples on the sample changer.

### LoKI

**Introduction to LoKI** ★: Tomas shows you LoKI (same page, a new first paragraph).

**How big are these nanoparticles?** Thread: Energy (Fyrø *Structures*: the blades).
- Request ★: the epoxy of the turbine blades is toughened with silica nanoparticles. A new batch must have a radius
  between 4.5 and 5.5 nm; an electron microscope sees a few hundred dried particles, SANS sees the billions of the
  batch, in the liquid. Measure the batch and decide whether it can be released.
- This replaces two different stories that contradict each other today: "medical imaging" (catalogue, BSc page, MSc
  notebook) and "a new polishing slurry" (proposal 3001). The samples and the numbers do not change; only the reason
  for the size limit changes (blood circulation → dispersion in the resin).
  **Decided (2026-11-16)**: the blades (one thread for the world); pages, notebook and proposal changed.
- Proposal 3001 ★: "Size of silica nanoparticles for toughened turbine-blade epoxy".

**Small structures in a membrane** (planned). Thread: Energy (Fyrø *Hydrogen plant*): the PEM membranes of the
electrolysers, contrast variation in H₂O/D₂O.
